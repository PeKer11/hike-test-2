# Data-source terms (checked 2026-10-26)

| Source | What the terms say | What Traike does |
|---|---|---|
| Transitous (transit) | transitous.org/api: only open-source, non-commercial apps; "Commercial use is not allowed"; best effort. | Live calls are OFF unless `TRANSIT_ENABLED=1` (dev only). The ride home reports "not available yet". Needs a commercial transit source, or written OK from Transitous (Matrix #transitous:matrix.spline.de). |
| openrouteservice (HeiGIT) | Read from account.heigit.org/info/tos (2026-10-26): no commercial-use ban. HeiGIT staff on the public forum, 2026-10-12 (ask.openrouteservice.org/t/8079): "commercial usage is allowed under the Standard Plan". Attribution must read "© openrouteservice by HeiGIT \| Data from OpenStreetMap". Results are licensed CC-BY-SA 4.0 (HeiGIT says it cannot advise on licence details). Prohibited conduct includes "Transmit personal data". One key per person, never in client code; limits 2,000 directions/day, 40/min. Staff 2026-08-27: results may be stored ("as long as you stick to our Terms of Service"). | Key is server-side only. Map credit uses the exact wording plus the OSM credit. Open risks: (1) start points can be a user's location, HeiGIT will not say if that is "personal data": we send coordinates only, no id/name, from our server. (2) CC-BY-SA ShareAlike on stored/shared route geometry: a lawyer question. |
| Google Places | Terms (Maps Service Terms, Places API s.10.3 + Places policies): place_id may be cached indefinitely; lat/lng at most 30 consecutive days; other Places content (rating, hours, accessibility) has no caching permission. Fixed: `expireCandidates` (src/lib/assistant/state.ts) drops Google content from stored chat state after 24h and the whole place after 30 days. | Done; strict reading (no hours kept at all) would break the opening-hours check across turns, a lawyer question. |

Wheelchair profile on api.heigit.org verified 2026-10-26: directions with the app's `profile_params.restrictions`, `weightings.green`, round trip, alternatives, matrix and `waytype/steepness/surface` extras all answer 200; an unroutable point answers HTTP 404 with code 2010 (handled as no-route).
