# Interactive Walk — ADDENDUM: Discovery Collection, tiered callouts, collapse & don't-repeat (Opus design, 2026-10-01)

Builds on `docs/INTERACTIVE_WALK_SPEC.md` (base spec). Reuse its modules (`walk-cadence.ts`, `walk-copy.ts`, `poi-relation.ts`, `rejoin.ts`, `/api/reroute`, `/api/nearby`, `NearbyProvider`, `PoiAnnouncer`, `PoiCallouts`, `runLocalRejoin`). Do not build a second POI system: the base-spec corridor fetch IS the registered tier of the Discovery Collection.

## 0. Answers
- Imagery NEVER runs on a timer. Discover once, ahead of time (plan / rebuild / collapse / coverage exit), store in a per-walk **Discovery Collection**, announce locally from it on every fix.
- Satellite land cover can't say "interesting" (ESA WorldCover 10 m, ~75% accuracy: tree/water/grass only). Evidence for "interesting" = registered data (OSM, Wikidata/Wikipedia) + crowd signal (Wikimedia Commons geotagged-photo density). Imagery = lowest tier, DEFERRED.
- 100 m is wrong both ways (too wide in a city, too narrow in parks) → tiered table below.
- "Too far" = >400 m from route sustained 60 s → collapse to walker's position, offer up to 3 direction options (nature / food & sights / shorter), each overlapping the walked track ≤25%, mutually different. ≤4 ORS directions calls, 0 matrix.
- Google: legal only with a Google basemap (Places content may not be shown with a non-Google map; Google imagery + ML forbidden). Spec the seam + activation checklist only; do NOT build Google now.

## 1. Legal sources
Use: OSM tags (ODbL, already), Wikipedia/Wikidata geosearch (free; `gsradius` 10–10000, `gslimit` ≤500), Commons photo density (`gsnamespace=6`; counting coords displays nothing), ESA WorldCover (CC-BY 4.0, free COGs, DEFER). No: Google satellite/Street View (never), Esri World Imagery (commercial needs paid sub), Mapbox/Maxar/Planet/Bing (derived-data/ML restrictions), GovMap ortho (needs written permission), Flickr (API commercial permission friction). Mapillary (CC-BY-SA): DEFER.
Research anchors: Quercia et al. beautiful routes are ~12% longer → detour budget anchor; Seresinhe 2018 (Flickr+OSM predict scenic ratings); Komoot ranks by crowd recommendation; Google "search along route" ranks by detour time → detour minutes is the primary cost metric.

## 2. Lateral display distances (constants; measured CROSS-TRACK from the route, not from the noisy fix)
| Env | See-it POI | See-it landmark/scenery/notable | Detour-offer limit (one-way) | Look-ahead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urban | 40 m | 80 m | 150 m | clamp(speed×45 s, 40, 100) |
| Park/green | 100 m | 200 m | 300 m | clamp(speed×45 s, 50, 120) |
| Rural/trail | 150 m | 400 m | 500 m | clamp(speed×60 s, 60, 150) |
Time rule overrides: offer detour only if `detourMin = 2*lateralM*1.25/speed_mpm <= min(8, max(3, 0.12*remainingWalkMin))`. Confidence: urban medium; park/rural low-medium → keep as tunable constants, tune from telemetry (shown→tapped→added→visited). Evidence: walking 1.35 m/s; urban GPS 7–13 m (15–50 m in canyons); pedestrian landmark studies use a 50 m buffer; willingness drops >400 m (5-min ped-shed); notification fatigue.

## 3. Discovery Collection
Types (`src/lib/types/discovery.ts`): `DiscoveryTier="registered"|"mapped-unnamed"|"crowd-signal"|"detected"` (also add "crowd-signal" to base `PlaceVerification`); `DiscoverySource="osm"|"wikidata"|"wikipedia"|"commons"|"worldcover"|"google"`; `DiscoveryState="new"|"announced"|"offered"|"dismissed"|"added"|"visited"`; `WalkEnvironment="urban"|"park"|"rural"`;
```ts
DiscoveryItem { id; attraction: Attraction; kind:"poi"|"scenery"; tier; sources[]; notable:boolean; photoCount?;
  score; frame:{alongM,lateralM,side:"left"|"right"|"on",segIdx}|null; detourMin; state }
DiscoveryCollection { items: Map<string,DiscoveryItem>; coverage: Coordinates[][]; ringRadiusM; routeVersion; scannedAtFixMs }
```
`src/lib/walk/discovery-collection.ts` (pure): `mergeItems` (match: same id → same wikidata QID → same normalised name within 50 m → crowd/detected within 50 m of registered is absorbed; tier only upgrades; state never regresses, dismissed stays dismissed); `reframe(col, geometry, cumDist, speedMpm, remainingWalkMin)`; `isCovered(col, path)` (≥90% of 20 m samples within ringRadius−150 of coverage); `scoreItem` (tierW {registered 1, mapped-unnamed .6, crowd .5, detected .3} × (1 + preferred + .8 notable + min(.6, log10(1+photos)/3)) − 0.08×detourMin); `nearby(col,pos,radiusM,exclude)`.
"Is it registered?" check for crowd/detected candidates: (1) local OSM element within 50 m / polygon bbox contains → merge; (2) Wikipedia geosearch page within 75 m same kind → registered; (3) Nominatim reverse zoom 17 (≤5 per scan, 1 req/s) names a tourism|leisure|natural|historic feature within 50 m → registered; (4) else keep tier + warning copy. Imagery is never proof.
Scan (one pass, extend `/api/nearby`, no new endpoint): one Overpass union query (corridor 250 m full tags + ring 600 m notable subset: tourism viewpoint|attraction|museum, historic, leisure park|nature_reserve|garden with name, natural peak|water|beach|wood, any `wikidata`); Wikipedia geosearch (`src/lib/places/wiki-geosearch.ts`, server-only; sample route every 1.5 km, ≤4 points; `he`+`en` inside Israel bbox; ≤8 calls; `generator=geosearch&ggsradius=800&ggslimit=50&prop=coordinates|pageprops&ppprop=wikibase_item`; User-Agent header; serial); Commons density (`commons-density.ts`; same points, radius 400 m, `gslimit=200`; ~50 m cells with ≥8 files → crowd candidate; SHOULD). Budget: 1 Overpass, ≤8 Wikipedia, ≤4 Commons; parallel, 8 s timeout each, merge as they arrive; p50 ≤3 s; never block route drawing.
Refresh triggers: plan shown/walk start → full scan; local rejoin splice → `isCovered` else scan only the uncovered sub-path (REPLACES base-spec "corridor refetch after splice"); full rebuild/collapse → same coverage test; walker > ring−150 m from coverage → point scan (reuse base `NEARBY_POINT_*` cache, ≥2 min gap); "Show me something different" → no network if ≥3 eligible unoffered items else point scan; TTL 60 min OSM/Wikipedia items.
Consumption: PoiAnnouncer fed by `eligibleForCallout(...)` (on route; cross-track frame) / existing `relatePlace` with heading (off route); deviation card may carry ONE line "While you're here: X · 120 m on your left" (registered, score ≥1); collapse + "something different" draw options from the collection. In-memory per walk (ref in WalkPlannerApp); Supabase persistence DEFER (`walk_discoveries` table with RLS own-rows, no names/coords stored).

## 4. Collapse & don't-repeat
`src/lib/walk/collapse-machine.ts` (pure, fix timestamps only): ON_ROUTE →(off >50 m & >accuracy)→ OFF_ROUTE →(30 s)→ REJOINING (`runLocalRejoin`) → success → ON_ROUTE. OFF_ROUTE|REJOINING → COLLAPSED if [deviation >400 m sustained 60 s] OR [2 rejoin failures/implausible] OR [off-route >8 min]. COLLAPSED → build options → OFFERING → user picks → ON_ROUTE (new plan, routeVersion++); "Back to my plan" → existing `runDeviationTriggeredRebuild`; walker returns within 30 m of old route → ON_ROUTE. Cooldown 10 min, max 4 per walk. `COLLAPSE_DEVIATION_M = LOCAL_REJOIN_MAX_DEVIATION_M` (replaces the base spec's >400 m fallback).
Exclusion set: visited stops; stops of the abandoned plan now behind; items `dismissed` or offered ×2; walked track (≤60 simplified points); abandoned plan's remaining geometry (soft).
`src/lib/walk/direction-options.ts` (pure): reach R=(remainingMin/2)·speed_mpm/1.3; exclude ±45° sector around came-from bearing (bearing to walked point 300 m back) unless endAnchor is there; themes Nature (scenery/park/viewpoint), Food & sights (food/museum/landmark/religious), Shorter (≤60% remaining time toward endAnchor/start); 6 sectors of 60°, sector score = sum of top-3 item scores, best sector per theme, no two options in the same/adjacent sector unless unavoidable; greedy stops while `Σ haversine·1.3/speed + visit ≤ budget`, ≤4 stops, order by brute force (≤24 perms) ending at endAnchor; no matrix, no tsp-planner change. Sparse fallbacks: Nature → one ORS call `options.round_trip{length: remainingMin·speed·0.8, points:3, seed:k}` + `profile_params.weightings.green:1` (only if no endAnchor or within 300 m); Shorter → origin→endAnchor with `alternative_routes{target_count:2, share_factor:0.5, weight_factor:1.6}` (2-waypoint only; pick least walked overlap). Builder: confirm `green` weighting works on our key with one manual call; drop it if ORS returns 400. ORS limits: alternatives/round trip ≤100 km, ≤3 alternatives; `avoid_polygons` ≤200 km² and ≤20 km extent.
`src/app/api/walk-options/route.ts` (orsRateLimiter, server-only): `POST {origin, endAnchor?, walked: Coordinates[≤60], options:[{theme, stops: Coordinates[≤4], mode:"via"|"roundtrip"|"alternatives", seed?}] (≤3)}`; one `getDirections` per option with all waypoints via `toOrsCoord`; add additive optional `options?: Record<string, unknown>` passthrough to `OrsDirectionsRequest`/`getDirections`; compute `overlapWalked` (and `overlapPlan` if plan abandoned); at most ONE retry across the whole request, for the worst option with `overlapWalked>0.25`, using `avoid_polygons` MultiPolygon of 20 m buffers around walked segments MINUS a disk of 80 m around origin and each stop (≤40 vertices per ring); drop options still >0.25 walked, or `overlapPlan>0.6` for non-"back" options; drop near-duplicates (pairwise overlap >0.5, keep higher score). Returns `{options:[{theme,geometry,distanceM,minutes,stops,overlapWalked}]}`; ≤4 directions calls, 0 matrix; 422 if nothing survives.
`src/lib/walk/route-overlap.ts`: `overlap(candidate, reference, {skipFirstM=100, stepM=20, tolM=25})` = fraction of 20 m samples on candidate within 25 m of reference; equirectangular local projection; use `cumulativeDistances` from walk-stats.
UI `src/components/walk/DirectionOptionsSheet.tsx`: in WalkHud bottom stack, replaces the alert card (max 1 card); title "You've gone a different way — pick a direction"; horizontally snapping row of ≤3 cards (theme icon, title, "45 min · 3.2 km · 3 stops", stop chips `dir="auto"`) + smaller pill "Back to my plan"; `bg-cream/95 backdrop-blur`, `text-forest`, selected card soft shadow + terra tint (no hard border); dashed preview polylines on map, tap card highlights its line. Choosing = existing `handleBuildWalk` with `explicitAttractions` = option stops, `keepViewport`, `autoResume`.

## 5. Single cadence table (principle: discover rarely and ahead of time; announce locally, often but gated)
GPS/ticker as base spec. Discovery scan: on plan/rebuild/collapse for uncovered geometry only; point scan outside coverage ≥2 min apart, 10-min cell cache. Registration check: ≤5 Nominatim/scan at 1/s. Imagery/WorldCover: never periodic (DEFER). Reframe: on every `routeVersion` change (local). Callout evaluation: every accepted fix, local only. TTL OSM/Wikipedia 60 min. Collapse: per fix, 10-min cooldown, ≤4/walk.

(3) Cache: place_id forever, lat/lng ≤30 days, nothing else; never write Google names/coords to Supabase or the shared Overpass cache; per-walk in-memory map. ## 6. Anti-overwhelm UX
New setting `calloutLevel: "quiet"|"normal"|"chatty"` (default normal) in `types/walk-settings.ts` + WalkSettingsPanel.
| Rule | Quiet | Normal | Chatty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global gap | 5 min | 90 s | 45 s |
| On screen | 1 | 2 (1 while an alert card is visible) | 2 |
| Per 10 min | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Category cooldown | 15 min | 10 min | 5 min |
| Tiers allowed | registered only; plan stops + notable/preferred | + mapped-unnamed scenery (≤1 per 10 min) | + crowd-signal (+ detected w/ warning) |
| Lateral multiplier | ×0.6 | ×1 | ×1.25 (detour limit unchanged) |
Always: no callouts within 30 m of a route turn (heading change >45° within 30 m); none within 100 m of an unvisited stop (except the stop); none while off-route/collapse card is up; none while "Paused" at a stop; when walker fast (>1.2× plan or >5.5 km/h) gap ×1.5 and lateral ×0.8; each place announced once per walk — collection `state` is the single source of truth.
